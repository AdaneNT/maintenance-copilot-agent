# Compressor Bearing High Temperature Response (synthetic)

> Illustrative document written for this demo. Not an official procedure.

## Alarm limits

- Normal bearing temperature for the gas compressor is 65 to 78 degrees C.
- High alarm at 85 degrees C.
- High-high trip at 95 degrees C.

## Response to a high alarm

1. Check lube oil supply temperature and pressure.
2. Check the lube oil cooler for fouling. Earlier excursions have been caused by cooler fouling.
3. Compare with vibration readings to separate lubrication problems from mechanical damage.
4. If the excursion was temporary, open an inspection work order and review oil analysis.

## Safety

Trip and alarm settings must not be changed or bypassed without an approved management of change process.
