# Vibration Severity Guideline (synthetic)

> Illustrative document written for this demo. Not an official procedure.

## Severity zones for medium-size rotating equipment

Overall velocity vibration (RMS, mm/s) is classified into four zones:

- Zone A (below 2.8 mm/s): newly commissioned machines, no action.
- Zone B (2.8 to 4.5 mm/s): acceptable for unrestricted long-term operation.
- Zone C (4.5 to 7.1 mm/s): not acceptable for long-term operation. Plan corrective maintenance and increase monitoring frequency to daily.
- Zone D (above 7.1 mm/s): vibration may cause damage. Notify the area technician immediately and evaluate controlled shutdown together with operations.

## Trend rules

A sustained increase of more than 25 percent over seven days is treated as a developing fault even when absolute values are still in zone B. Typical root causes are bearing wear, misalignment, imbalance and cavitation.
