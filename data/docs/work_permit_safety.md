# Work Permit and Safety Principles (synthetic)

> Illustrative document written for this demo. Not an official procedure.

## Principles

- Safety has priority over production.
- Intrusive maintenance requires a work permit, isolation and verification of zero energy.
- Safety systems such as trips, interlocks and fire and gas detection must never be bypassed without formal approval.
- Decisions on shutdown are made by operations together with the responsible technician, never by a software assistant alone.

## Role of digital assistants

Digital assistants may summarise data and procedures and suggest next steps. They do not execute actions on equipment and their recommendations must be verified by qualified personnel.
