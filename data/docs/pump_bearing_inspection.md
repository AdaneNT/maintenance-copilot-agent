# Centrifugal Pump Bearing Inspection (synthetic)

> Illustrative document written for this demo. Not an official procedure.

## When to perform

Perform a bearing inspection when vibration enters zone C, when bearing temperature rises more than 10 degrees C above its normal operating level, or when abnormal noise is reported.

## Steps

1. Confirm the trend with a second measurement, including spectrum analysis if available.
2. Check lubrication: oil level, oil condition and grease interval.
3. Check alignment between pump and driver.
4. If bearing defect frequencies are present in the spectrum, schedule bearing replacement.
5. Record findings in a corrective work order.

## Safety

All intrusive work requires a valid work permit and isolation of the equipment. Never perform intrusive work on running equipment.
