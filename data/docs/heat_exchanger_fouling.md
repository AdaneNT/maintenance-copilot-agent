# Heat Exchanger Fouling Monitoring (synthetic)

> Illustrative document written for this demo. Not an official procedure.

## Indicators

Fouling shows as a gradual drop in heat transfer, a rising pressure drop across the bundle and reduced flow at constant pump speed.

## Actions

- Trend the pressure drop weekly.
- Plan tube bundle cleaning when the pressure drop exceeds 1.5 times the clean value.
- Chemical cleaning may be used when mechanical cleaning is not possible.
